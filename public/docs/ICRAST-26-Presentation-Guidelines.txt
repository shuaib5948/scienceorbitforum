SCIENCE ORBIT FORUM
INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON RECENT ADVANCEMENTS IN SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY 2026 (ICRAST-26)
In collaboration with the Prof. M.S. Swaminathan Chair for Science Popularisation and Research, University of Calicut

================================================================================
PRESENTATION GUIDELINES, TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Theme: "Biotechnology and Frontier Sciences for the Betterment of Human Life"
Date: 21 November 2026 | Venue: University of Calicut – Aryabhata Hall | Mode: Hybrid Mode
================================================================================

1. General Guidelines
• These guidelines apply to participants whose abstracts have been accepted for oral or poster presentation at ICRAST-26.
• Presenters must follow the presentation category, schedule, technical instructions and other communications issued by the organising team.
• Acceptance of an abstract does not, by itself, guarantee selection for an award or publication.
• The organisers will communicate the final presentation schedule and any necessary instructions to accepted presenters.

2. Abstract Acceptance and Presentation Mode
• Presenters should rely on the official acceptance communication to confirm that their abstract has been accepted and to identify the assigned presentation category.
• Presenters must confirm their participation and presentation mode (onsite or online) as requested by the organisers.
• Any request to change the presentation mode or presenter should be communicated to the organising team as early as possible. Approval will depend on feasibility and the final schedule.

3. Oral Presentation Guidelines
• Each oral presentation will be allocated a total of 15 minutes: 10 minutes for the presentation followed by 5 minutes for questions and answers.
• Presenters should prepare their slides in a standard presentation format and ensure that the material is readable and suitable for scientific discussion.
• Presenters should arrive or connect in advance of their assigned session and have their presentation file ready when called.
• Presenters are expected to keep within the allotted time so that the programme can proceed as scheduled. The session chair will manage the session and Q&A.

4. Poster Presentation Guidelines
• The required poster size is A0. Presenters are responsible for printing their posters and bringing the printed copy to the venue.
• Posters should present the research clearly and concisely, with an appropriate title, author names and affiliations, background or objectives, methods, key results, conclusions and relevant references, as applicable to the work.
• Each poster presentation will be allocated 5 minutes for presentation followed by 3 minutes for questions and answers.
• Presenters should display their posters in the location and during the period communicated by the organisers, and should be available for discussion during their assigned presentation slot.
• Any specific orientation, mounting or display instructions will be communicated by the organising team if required.

5. Online Presentation Guidelines
• Online presentations will run in parallel with the onsite programme and will follow the same presentation durations and Q&A allocation as the corresponding presentation category.
• Online oral presenters will have 10 minutes to present and 5 minutes for Q&A. Online poster presenters will have 5 minutes to present and 3 minutes for Q&A.
• Online presenters must deliver their presentations live at the assigned time and join the meeting using the access details and instructions provided by the organisers.
• Presenters are responsible for arranging a stable internet connection, a functioning microphone and a suitable device. They should connect early enough to check audio, screen sharing and presentation files.
• If technical difficulties arise, the presenter should notify the organising team promptly. Any rescheduling or alternative arrangement will be subject to the organisers' decision and programme constraints.

6. Attendance and Presenter Responsibilities
• Presenters must be present or connected at the assigned time and remain available for the full presentation and Q&A period.
• Presenters should communicate respectfully with session chairs, reviewers, fellow presenters and attendees.
• The presenting author is responsible for ensuring that the presentation accurately represents the submitted work and that any co-author or institutional details are correct.
• If a presenter is unable to attend, they should inform the organising team as early as possible. The organisers will determine whether an alternative presenter or arrangement can be accommodated.

7. Authorship, Originality and Research Ethics
• The submitted work must be the original work of the stated author(s), or must clearly acknowledge relevant contributions and sources.
• All authors and contributors should be appropriately credited. Presenters are responsible for obtaining any permissions required to use third-party figures, images, data or other material.
• Plagiarism, fabricated or falsified results, misrepresentation of authorship, and other forms of research misconduct are not acceptable.
• Research involving human participants, animals, confidential information or other regulated material should comply with applicable ethical requirements and permissions.
• The organisers may seek clarification about a submission or take appropriate action if credible concerns about originality, authorship or research ethics arise.

8. Best Presenter Awards and Evaluation
• ICRAST-26 will have a Best Presenter Award for the oral presentation category and a Best Presenter Award for the poster presentation category.
• Presentations will be evaluated by a committee designated by the organisers. The committee may consider factors such as scientific content, clarity, organisation, presentation skills and responses to questions, as appropriate to the category.
• The decision of the session chair will be final. The organisers' announcement of the results will conclude the award decision process.

9. Certificates
• Certificates will be awarded to presenters who actually deliver their accepted oral or poster presentation, whether onsite or online.
• Certificate details will be based on the information submitted by the presenter. Presenters should notify the organisers promptly of any corrections needed to their name, affiliation or presentation title.
• The organisers will communicate the method and timeline for certificate distribution.

10. Possible Publication of Selected Works
• Selected works may be considered for publication after review by the conference committee and a peer-review process, subject to editorial approval and confirmation of publication arrangements.
• Selection for presentation at ICRAST-26 does not guarantee selection for publication. Authors may be asked to submit a full paper and make revisions in accordance with instructions issued separately.
• Any publication will be subject to the required author permissions, originality checks, editorial requirements and applicable publication policies.
• The organisers intend to pursue appropriate ISBN and DOI arrangements for the relevant publication, but the assignment of an ISBN or DOI, the publication date, and any indexing status are not guaranteed unless formally confirmed by the relevant provider or publisher.
• Presenters should not assume that an abstract or presentation will be published automatically. Detailed submission, review, licensing and publication instructions will be communicated if a publication process is confirmed.

11. Programme Changes and Organisers' Authority
• The organising team reserves the right to make reasonable changes to the schedule, session arrangements, presentation sequence or technical format when necessary.
• Any material changes will be communicated to presenters through the available official communication channels.
• The session chair will manage the session, including timekeeping and Q&A. Presenters are expected to follow the chair's instructions and the reasonable directions of the organising team.

12. Contact and Further Instructions
• For questions about abstract acceptance, presentation mode, schedules, technical arrangements or these guidelines, presenters should contact the ICRAST-26 organising team through the official conference communication channel.
